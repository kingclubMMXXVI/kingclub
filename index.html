The idea
“A king is not defined by what he possesses, but by what he commands of himself.”

The King Club is deliberately selective. It is not a popularity contest, a networking gimmick, or a place to collect titles.

Membership is earned through conduct. We bring together men who want strong friendships, meaningful competition, useful conversation, and an environment that rewards excellence.

What we expect

The Standard

Four principles. No shortcuts.

I

Character

Keep your word. Treat people with respect. Your reputation is built when nobody is watching.

II

Discipline

Do difficult things on purpose. Train your mind, your body, and your ability to follow through.

III

Brotherhood

Challenge your friends without tearing them down. Celebrate their wins and show up when it matters.

IV

Ambition

Have something worth pursuing. Improve relentlessly, and bring the people around you upward.

V

Composure

Confidence without arrogance. Strength without cruelty. Know when to speak—and when to listen.

VI

Service

Leadership is responsibility. Use your abilities to make your circle, school, community, or craft better.
